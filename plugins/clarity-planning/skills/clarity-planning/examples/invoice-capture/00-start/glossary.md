# Glossary

## In plain words

These are the terms clerks and managers actually use. "Match" and "post" are the two most often confused with each other.

| Term | Means to the users | Record or trace it leaves | Not to be confused with |
|---|---|---|---|
| Invoice | The supplier's bill, as a PDF | A stored PDF plus the fields we read from it | The purchase order |
| PO (purchase order) | What the company ordered and agreed to pay | A PO with lines in the finance app | An invoice; a PO can have several invoices |
| Match | Pairing an invoice with its supplier and the PO lines it bills | The match artifact (ART-02) | Posting |
| Coding | Choosing the accounting codes (account, cost centre) for each line | The coding artifact (ART-03) | Approval |
| GRN (goods received note) | Proof that goods arrived | A receipt record, for about 70% of deliveries | A PO |
| Post | Sending the approved invoice to the ERP | The posting record (ART-05) | Match, or approval |
| Void (an approval) | An approval that no longer counts because something it covered changed | The approval returns to the clerk | Reject, which is a manager's decision |
