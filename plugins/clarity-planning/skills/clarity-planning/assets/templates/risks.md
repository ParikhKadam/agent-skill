# Risks and unknowns

## In plain words

List the assumptions the design rests on and ask of each: if this is wrong, how much breaks (impact 1-3), and how sure are we (uncertainty 1-3, where 3 means we are guessing)? Score is impact times uncertainty. Test the top one to three before building. A good spike has a measurable bar and a fallback design if it fails.

Plan is `spike` (link the spike doc), `accept` (say why it is cheap to be wrong) or `mitigate` (say how). Status is `open`, `tested` or `accepted`.
