# Constraints

## In plain words

Teams treat their own earlier choices as if they were laws. Sort every limit into **imposed** (an outside system, a regulation, a contract: we cannot change it) or **chosen** (a past decision or a coded rule: we may redesign it when something better is measured). Only imposed constraints bound the design.
